TRADITIONAL ART FORMS

Art that tells the story of Andhra.

Kuchipudi

A classical dance form from Andhra Pradesh, known for its grace, expressions and storytelling

Tholu Bommalata

A traditional shadow puppet art form that brings stories to life.

Etikoppala Toys

World famous wooden toys handcrafted by skilled artisans of Etikoppaka.

Kalamkari

A unique hand-painting and block-printing art, known for its intricate designs.
